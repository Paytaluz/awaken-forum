What we're building next on the tech side

Tech Team Oct 29, 2026 3 min read

Not every build is public-facing. Some of the most useful things Tech ships never leave the building — and that's by design.

#Tech #Product

Plenty of what Tech builds is meant to be seen: the sites, the tools talent and clients click through, the systems behind a campaign microsite. But a growing share of the roadmap is internal — dashboards, scrapers, small admin tools that never get a public URL but save someone on another team hours every week.

One recent example: a lightweight internal database for tracking talent profiles and their public reach across platforms, built so the PR and Digital teams can look someone up in seconds instead of hunting across spreadsheets. Nobody outside the company will ever see its interface, and it's still one of the higher-impact things shipped this quarter.

Boring infrastructure, on purpose

We try to resist the pull toward building the most interesting thing on the list. A reliable deploy pipeline, consistent redirects, a backup that actually restores cleanly — none of it is exciting, and all of it is what makes the exciting projects possible without last-minute fire drills.

"The best infrastructure work is invisible. You only notice it on the one day it isn't there."

So the roadmap keeps a standing slot for unglamorous fixes alongside the bigger builds — because the small tool that saves one team an hour a week adds up faster than most people expect.
